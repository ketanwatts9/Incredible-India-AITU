# 🇮🇳 India @ Cultural Day — Astana IT University

> An interactive website built to represent **India** at the **Cultural Day** event hosted by Astana IT University, Kazakhstan — displayed live at the Indian canopy during the celebration.

---

## 🌍 Background

During my semester exchange at **Astana IT University (AITU), Astana, Kazakhstan**, the university organized a **Cultural Day** where students from different countries represented their home culture.

As part of the **Indian delegation**, we set up a canopy showcasing Indian culture — and this website was built and **run live at the canopy** to give visitors an engaging, visual experience of India's rich heritage.

---

## 🎯 What the Website Showcases

- 🗺️ Overview of India — geography, diversity, and people
- 🎭 Culture — festivals, traditions, and art forms
- 🍛 Food — iconic Indian dishes and cuisine
- 👗 Traditional clothing — regional attire across India
- 🎶 Music & Dance — classical and folk forms
- 🏛️ Famous landmarks and monuments

---

## 💡 Purpose

This was not a college assignment — it was built with a **real use case**:  
Running on a laptop/screen at our canopy so AITU students and visitors could explore India interactively during the Cultural Day event.
